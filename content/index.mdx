# Welcome to VectorVerify

VectorVerify is a web platform for reviewing, validating, and monitoring
mosquito surveillance data collected by field teams using the VectorCam mobile
application.

## What does VectorVerify do?

Field teams collect mosquito specimens and household survey data in the field.
VectorVerify gives program staff a central place to:

- **Review** surveillance data submitted each month before it enters national
  reporting systems
- **Annotate** mosquito tasks
- **Monitor** operational activity across surveillance sites and districts

## Who is it for?

VectorVerify is used by expert reviewers, program managers, district
supervisors, and researchers involved in mosquito surveillance programs. Access
to features depends on your assigned role — see [User Roles](/docs/user-roles)
for details.
