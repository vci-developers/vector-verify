# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the landing page after you log in. It provides a high-level
overview of surveillance activity across your program.

## What you'll see

- **Summary metrics** — total sessions submitted, specimens collected, and
  active sites for the selected period
- **Pending review work** — sites and months that still need review or
  certification
- **Flagged records** — submissions that have been marked as incomplete or
  conflicting
- **Activity trends** — charts showing surveillance activity over time
- **Site-level visibility** — a breakdown of activity per sentinel site

## Filters

Use the filters at the top of the dashboard to narrow the view by program, site,
or date range. The metrics and charts update automatically when filters change.

## Quick navigation

The dashboard includes direct links into the [Review](/docs/review) and
[Annotation](/docs/annotation) workflows so you can jump straight to work that
needs attention.
