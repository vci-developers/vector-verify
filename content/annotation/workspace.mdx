# Annotation Workspace

The Annotation Workspace is where you label individual specimens within a task.
It is designed for focused, efficient annotation — you move through specimens
one at a time and submit labels as you go.

![The Annotation Workspace showing a specimen image and the annotation form](/docs/annotation/form-specimen.png)

## Labeling a specimen

For each specimen, record what you see in the image:

| Field              | Options                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Genus**          | Anopheles, Aedes, Culex, Mansonia, Non-Mosquito, Unknown                                   |
| **Species**        | For _Anopheles_: gambiae, funestus, other, unknown                                         |
| **Sex**            | Female, Male, Unknown                                                                      |
| **Abdomen status** | Unfed, Fully fed, Gravid, Unknown                                                          |
| **Artifacts**      | Blurry Image, Missing Specimen Parts, Bad Specimen Pose, Multiple Specimens Visible, Other |
| **Notes**          | Free-text observations                                                                     |

The form adapts to your selections: species is only requested for _Anopheles_,
and sex and abdomen status are skipped for non-mosquito specimens (abdomen
status is also skipped for males). Once the required fields are filled in, click
**Submit** to record your annotation.

Start by choosing the **Genus**:

![The genus dropdown](/docs/annotation/form-genus.png)

For _Anopheles_ specimens, choose the **Species**:

![The species dropdown](/docs/annotation/form-species.png)

Then record the **Sex** and **Abdomen status**:

![The sex dropdown](/docs/annotation/form-sex.png)

![The abdomen status dropdown](/docs/annotation/form-abdomen.png)

Finally, note any image **Artifacts**:

![The artifacts dropdown](/docs/annotation/form-artifacts.png)

## Flagging a specimen

Use the **Flag** toggle when a specimen cannot be confidently identified. You
can also use the **Notes** field to record any observations about the specimen.

## Progress indicator

The header always shows your current position in the task (e.g. "42 of 100
annotated") so you can track how much work remains.
