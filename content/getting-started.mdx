# Getting Started

## Logging in

Navigate to the VectorVerify URL provided by your program administrator. Enter
your email and password, then click **Sign In**.

If you have not created an account, create account by clicking sign up. You will
need to wait for you account to be whitlisted before you can countinue.

## Navigating the platform

Once logged in, you will see the main navigation in the sidebar. The pages
available to you depend on your [access level](/docs/user-roles):

| Page           | What it's for                                                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard**  | High-level summary of surveillance activity and program status |
| **Review**     | Monthly site-level data validation workflow                    |
| **Annotation** | Expert annotation of mosquito specimen images                  |
| **Operations** | Operational monitoring across sites and districts              |
