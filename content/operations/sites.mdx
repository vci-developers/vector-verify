# Sites Overview

The Sites Overview tab gives you a site-by-site picture of surveillance activity
for the selected district and time frame.

## The site list

Each sentinel site in the filtered view shows:

- **Site name** and district
- **Sessions submitted** — how many collection sessions were recorded
- **Specimens collected** — total specimen count for the period
- **Last submission date** — when the most recent data was recorded
- **Collector count** — number of distinct field workers who submitted data

## Expanding a site

Click any site to expand it and see a more detailed breakdown:

- **Surveillance form summary** — key responses across sessions (collection
  method, indoor/outdoor, etc.)
- **Specimen summary** — specimen count with an optional species breakdown

## Common use cases

- Identify sites that have submitted no data (inactive sites)
- Compare activity levels between sites in the same district
- Spot sites with unusually low specimen counts that may need follow-up
- Drill into a specific site to check data completeness
