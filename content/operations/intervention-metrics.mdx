# Intervention Metrics

The Intervention Metrics tab shows bednet (LLIN) coverage across surveyed
households for the selected locations and date range.

<Callout type="info">This tab is only available for Uganda programs.</Callout>

## What you'll see

- **Net Usage Rate** — the percentage of surveyed people who slept under a net,
  out of everyone surveyed across inspected households. Also shows **People Not
  Under Net** and **Households Inspected** as supporting counts. If no nets were
  distributed in the selected district, this card shows "No nets distributed in
  this district" instead of a percentage.
- **People per Net** — the ratio of total people who slept under a net to the
  total nets available, along with the **Nets Available** count.
- **Fed Anopheles / People Slept Under Net** — fully fed Anopheles mosquitoes
  collected divided by total number of people who slept under a net, along with
  individual counts of **Fed Anopheles** and **People Slept Under Net**.

## Using the filters

The view responds to the shared Operations filters. Select a location and time
frame to see net usage and coverage for that area and period.
