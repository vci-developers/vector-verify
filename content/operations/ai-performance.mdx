# AI Performance

The AI Performance tab shows how well the machine learning model is performing
based on expert annotations submitted through the [Annotation](/docs/annotation)
workflow.

![The AI Performance tab with coverage and the species confusion matrix](/docs/operations/03-ai-performance.png)

<Callout type="info">
    This tab requires annotation data to be populated. If no annotations have
    been submitted for the selected period, the tab will show no results.
</Callout>

## Confusion matrices

The tab shows a confusion matrix for each of **species**, **sex**, and **abdomen
status**. Each matrix compares the model's predictions against the expert
annotations:

- **Rows** — the visual verification label (what the annotator said)
- **Columns** — the VectorCam label (what the model predicted)
- **Cells** — how often that label/prediction combination occurred

A well-performing model will have large numbers along the diagonal (correct
predictions) and small numbers elsewhere.

## Accuracy metrics

- **Accuracy** — the percentage of specimens classified correctly, shown for each
  matrix
- **Per-label metrics** — sensitivity and specificity broken down by individual
  label, so you can see which the model handles well and which it struggles with

## Coverage

The tab also shows review coverage — how many specimens have been reviewed
(annotated or flagged) out of the total for the selected period. Low coverage
means the accuracy metrics are based on a small sample and may not be
representative.
