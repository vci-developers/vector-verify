# VHT Compliance

VHT stands for Village Health Team — the field workers who collect mosquito
specimens at sentinel sites. The VHT Compliance tab monitors field team activity
to ensure data is being collected consistently and coverage targets are being
met.

## Activity metrics

- Sessions submitted per VHT
- Specimens collected per VHT
- Number of active VHTs over the selected period

## Coverage metrics

- Households visited
- Sessions per site
- Sessions per district

## Compliance metrics

- Days with submissions — how many days a VHT submitted data
- Missed collection days — scheduled collection days with no submission
- Average sessions per week

## Performance metrics

- Specimens per session — whether VHTs are collecting the expected number of
  specimens per visit
- Incomplete session rate — sessions submitted with missing data
- Review issues per VHT — how often a VHT's submissions require correction
  during review

## Using this tab

Use the district filter to narrow to a specific area. You can identify VHTs who
are underperforming or sites with insufficient coverage, and follow up with
field supervisors accordingly.
