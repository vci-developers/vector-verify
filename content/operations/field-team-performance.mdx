# Field Team Performance

The Field Team Performance tab monitors the field workers — collectors — who
gather mosquito specimens at sentinel sites, so you can see who is active and how
consistently data is being collected.

![The Field Team Performance tab](/docs/operations/04-field-team-performance.png)

## What you'll see

- **District Collectors** — the total number of collectors in the district
- **Active Collectors** — how many submitted data during the selected period
- A **collector activity grid** — one row per collector, with their field team,
  showing activity across each month of the selected range

## Using the filters

The view responds to the shared Operations filters. Select a district and time
frame to see the collectors and their activity for that area and period.
