# Certification

Certification is the final step of the Review workflow. It confirms that all
review work for the selected site and month is complete and that the data is
ready for submission to national surveillance systems.

## Before you can certify

Both earlier steps must be completed first:

- Surveillance questions reviewed and conflicts resolved
- All specimens approved, corrected, or flagged

The certification page shows a summary of the review work so you can confirm
everything is in order before proceeding.

## The certification summary

| Item               | Details                                   |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Site               | The sentinel site being certified         |
| Month              | The reporting month                       |
| Sessions reviewed  | Number of sessions included in the review |
| Specimens reviewed | Total specimens assessed                  |
| Outstanding flags  | Any remaining flagged items               |

## Certifying the dataset

If you are satisfied with the review, click **Certify** to complete the process.
You can also add an optional note explaining any decisions or exceptions made
during the review.

<Callout type="warning">
    Certification is recorded with your name and a timestamp. It cannot be
    undone without contacting your program administrator.
</Callout>

## After certification

The dataset is marked as certified and flagged as ready for submission to
national reporting systems such as DHIS2. The site's status on the
[Site & Month Selection](/docs/review/site-selection) page updates to
**Certified**.
