# Certification

Certification is the final step of the Review workflow. It confirms that all
review work for the selected site and collection cycle is complete and that the
data is ready for submission to national surveillance systems.

![The certification confirmation screen](/docs/review-detail/03-certification.png)

## Before you can certify

Both earlier steps must be completed first:

- Surveillance questions reviewed and conflicts resolved
- All specimens approved

## Certifying the dataset

If you are satisfied with the review, click **Certify** to complete the process.
You can also add an optional note explaining any decisions or exceptions made
during the review.

<Callout type="warning">
    Certification is recorded with your name and a timestamp. This process
    cannot be undone.
</Callout>

## After certification

Once certified, the site can be submitted to DHIS2 from the
[DHIS2 Submissions](/docs/review/submissions) tab.
