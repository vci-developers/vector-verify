# Site & Month Selection

The Site & Month Selection page is the entry point for the Review workflow. It
lets you choose which sentinel site and reporting month to work on.

## Selecting a month

Use the **Month** and **Year** selectors at the top of the page to set the
reporting period. All site information below will update to reflect data for
that month.

## The site list

The page displays all sentinel sites available to you. For each site you can
see:

- **Site name** and district
- **Number of sessions** submitted that month
- **Review status** — whether the site is unreviewed, in progress, or certified

Sites are grouped or sorted so that sites needing attention appear first.

## Starting or resuming a review

Click a site to begin or continue reviewing it. If a review is already in
progress, you will be taken directly to the step where you left off.

## What the statuses mean

| Status                  | What to do                                  |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Not reviewed            | Click the site to begin the review workflow |
| In progress             | Click to resume where you left off          |
| Ready for certification | All review steps done — proceed to certify  |
| Certified               | Review complete; no action needed           |

Once you select a site and month, you move to the
[Surveillance Questions](/docs/review/surveillance-questions) step.
