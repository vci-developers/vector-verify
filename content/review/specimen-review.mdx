# Specimen Review

The Specimen Review step lets you quickly validate the mosquito specimens
collected during the collection cycle.

![Reviewing a specimen image alongside its model prediction](/docs/review-detail/02-image-review.png)

## The review workspace

Each specimen is displayed one at a time with:

- The **specimen image** for visual inspection
- The **machine learning prediction** — species, sex, and abdomen status
- **Basic metadata** — household ID, collection date, collector, site

## Navigating between specimens

Use the **Next** and **Previous** buttons to move through specimens one at a
time, or use the keyboard shortcuts displayed on screen for faster navigation.

## Completing this step

Once you have verified all specimens are correct, click continue to
certification to move to the final step,
[Certification](/docs/review/certification).
